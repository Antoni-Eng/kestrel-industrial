# Incident Report IR-003

Document type: incident
Equipment: CNC-311
Plant: PLANT-NORTH
Date: 2026-06-06

## Summary
During shift handover, an operator reported abnormal noise on CNC-311. Production paused. Maintenance found early bearing wear. No injury occurred.

## Immediate Actions
- Machine locked out per SOP-LOTO-01
- Temporary production moved to alternate asset
- Bearing replacement scheduled under WO linked to CNC-311

## Corrective Actions
- Replace bearing pack per manual for CNC-311
- Increase vibration walkaround frequency for 30 days
- Review lubrication route compliance
